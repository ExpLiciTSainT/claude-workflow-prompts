# A decision put to me (the form)

Use for: anything that is mine to decide (look, feel, a number I set, money, anything irreversible).
Why: a decision I cannot act on in one glance costs a round trip; this form has everything in one read.

```text
When a decision is mine, put it in this form and nothing else:
- WHAT: one sentence, in plain words, with the picture or number that shows it (full path).
- WHY NOW: what waits on it, one sentence.
- YES costs: one sentence (what changes, what it removes, what it costs).
- NO costs: one sentence (what stays wrong or slower).
- Your recommendation, one sentence, if you have one; never the method.
- Never an option that changes the product only for a recording or a test: the change is made in what I launch, or it is not offered.
- My answer is the go: the work it unblocks starts in the same turn; never wait for a separate "proceed".
If there are two options, show them side by side in ONE image, mine on the left and the new one on the right, labelled inside the image, the measured numbers under each.
```
