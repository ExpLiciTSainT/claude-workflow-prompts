# Review and fix loop (AGY, Opus, Grok on every hand-back; fix once; close on the second failure)

Use for: every hand-back, before the orchestrator builds on it or shows it to me.
Why: an independent eye on the real output caught what the lane's own reads passed (a skip, a frozen arm, a replayed render); a bounded loop stops it costing more than the work; and a test only proves the mechanism it tests, never what I see.

```text
Review this hand-back before anything is built on it or shown to me. The reviewers by the kind of hand-back, each on the real output, not the lane's description of it, spawned from the top level in one message (in two windows, by the driver), never from inside a lane. An item for me or a checkpoint (a video, a sheet, a still, a pointer): AGY first, then Opus and Grok. A code hand-back (a read, a tool, a fix with no picture): Opus and Grok, AGY on any picture or video it carries. A plan: Opus and Grok on the text, AGY on its pictures. A process, docs or prompts hand-back: one Opus read. Research (a tool, a vendor claim): AGY and Grok first, one Opus summary with sources. Three on everything is retired (2026-11-07: three reads on a process packet found the same faults three times at two thirds of the lane's tokens; where each eye sees something different, all three stay):

1. AGY (Gemini), the motion and picture eye, by the agy command (its command line and raw output path in its return, or the review is refused), after the DINO screen: the held frames from every view, and for anything that moves, STRIPS of consecutive frames at speed (10 a second through a whole cycle, every view) cut from the video, plus the video file itself where the tool accepts video; the one question "what is wrong, ranked by body part and view; does it read as a real person; where does it not?". Its read is recorded beside the item. AGY's visual finding is never closed on Opus's or the lane's word: whoever disputes it puts the frame or crop beside the dispute and the orchestrator looks at it; a finding with no frame against it stands and goes to me in the pointer as AGY's catch with its crop (I have seen Opus call AGY's real catch made up, and AGY was right). Its figures (a pixel, a frame, a count) are pointers under item 4; what it says the picture shows is not a figure.
2. Opus 5.5 at high, adversarial: the same pictures and strips, the numbers table, the hand-back's claims against its files; it tries to refute what the lane claims. On a gate (a read, a check, a server rule, a plan change) it runs at xhigh.
3. Grok, the code and numbers eye, by the grok command (its command line and raw output path in its return, or the review is refused): the files changed, the reads, the tests, the settings against the vendor's own documentation; findings with file and line (pictures too where its CLI takes images).
4. Reviewers point, lanes measure: a reviewer's pixel, frame or count is a pointer; the lane measures it on the file before it accepts or disputes it. No per-finding verifiers; a verified review only when the orchestrator rules a gate needs one. A finding marked blocker names the bar it fails (a rule, a brief line, a number against its bar) or the frame or crop that shows it; without either the orchestrator rules it major in one line, no dispute needed.
5. The SAME lane, once, answers every finding (accepted with the change made, or disputed at file and line with evidence): that is its one fix pass. The orchestrator rules only the disputes still standing, from the compact tables; it does not re-read the lane's full output. A later fix round is a fresh lane from the records on disk; AGY re-reads the fixed pictures and strips.
6. A second failure of the same outcome closes the method: the orchestrator decides the next method or states the fault as known on the next pointer to me; never a third try of the same thing.
7. A test proves a mechanism; it never replaces the holistic look. Every hand-back gets the picture and strip review even when its tests pass, and I see the whole thing moving at the review points, because what I experience is the only bar that counts.
8. Nothing reaches my review folder until its reviewers have read it and the lane has answered: the lane stages items in its hand-back folder and they are placed after the ruling; the pointer lists what is known wrong so I judge the rest.
9. Before a brief runs, one free reviewer reads it (AGY for a look or motion brief, Grok for a code or read brief) against the sources it cites: which step builds on something the brief has not checked, which source does it misread, what does my wording ask that it leaves out. The orchestrator amends the brief, then runs it. Not for a fix brief under ten lines.
```
