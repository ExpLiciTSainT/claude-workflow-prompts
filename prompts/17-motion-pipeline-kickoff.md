# The motion kickoff (paste into the director window for a new character's movement)

Use for: the one message that starts a character's movement set in the director window, after the "Two windows" preset; the director then runs every step in order so none is found late. The fighting motions have their own message, block 18.
Why: the male's round found its recording route, room, camera, stance method, image quota and review shape one phase at a time; each is now a step with its guard before it (Torvane Docs/process/humanoid-fighter-motion-pipeline.md, HU-130 and HU-131).

```text
MOTION KICKOFF. Character: <character name>. Review folder: <review folder>. Keys file: <keys file path>. Reference folder: <folder with my pictures and videos of how he stands and moves>. My words: <what I want, in my words>.

Director, in this order; from step 3 on each step is a phase you send by "run", its brief naming its HU-130 step, that step's guard rows and its shares-with and starts-when lines; a phase that shares nothing with a running one runs beside it:
1. Create <project> (block 16) and append my words to REQUEST.md verbatim. Read the pipeline doc (HU-130, HU-131) and the repeat-mistakes rows it names.
2. PLAN.md from the full inventory of the finished set: every move, its keys from the keys file (a key the game does not bind yet is named now), every consumer of the stance and the carriage, nothing I did not ask for. Every time I named in a reviewed video is written with the move from that video's index; every numeric bar with its derivation on this body.
3. The stance reference, the plan's one input made before its review: a stand on a realistic human made from my words, the image tool's quota and its fallback named before step 1, the generator looking at every image, Grok never asked for images of the bare character; the sheet to my review folder. My pick is the go.
4. The plan: a fresh planner lane, round 1 on the plan and round 2 on the method, each round run by the driver (an Opus adversarial and Grok by the grok command on the text; AGY by the agy command on the plan's pictures only); the planner answers every finding; you rule the disputes.
5. The recording route before any recording: only the keys, the mouse and the start place set by the route; both soles and the floor under them in frame by the game's own camera; the room in the game's own data, which my launcher reads; rebuild before record; the record line complete (build, DINO's result, AGY's read and verdict, the bar values); frame pacing measured; DINO, then AGY's standing items, which answer "not in frame" for what the frame does not show, calibrated (a new character "uncalibrated" until my first verdict on it); a test recording that runs every bar live.
6. Then: the character posed to my pick in the visible window by the MCP tools and shown as engine stills in the map from every view (my yes); the living idle; the carriage still from every view (my yes); the carriage layer; the shared fixes' plans reviewed as their own phase, then the shared fixes coded with magnitude bars passing on live recordings; one video per motion in my review folder (my verdict on each); the checkpoint.
Nothing I did not ask for starts without my yes. Report what is done and what is next, never a date.
```
